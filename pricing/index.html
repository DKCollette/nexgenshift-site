FAQ

Common questions.

Are Starter prices fixed?

Yes — Starter monthly and annual prices shown are approved public prices. Growth and Premium are scoped with you and confirmed in a fixed written quote before we build.

How does annual billing save 23%?

Annual is a single upfront payment covering 12 months at 77% of the monthly-billed yearly total. Same plan, lower effective monthly cost.

How does the Small-Business Website promotion work?

Qualifying standard small-business websites get a $0 (FREE) promotional build (regularly $750) plus the first subscription month free at $50/month. The first recurring payment starts in month two, measured from your website launch / subscription activation. No payments are taken online — billing is handled manually after launch. Custom applications, extensive integrations, specialized functionality, and complex development are quoted separately.

What don't the discounts apply to?

Promotional discounts apply to development only. They never apply to AWS bills, hosting fees, third-party services or licensing, taxes, or recurring subscriptions.

Project development fee vs. everything else?

The development fee pays for design and build work. Hosting is where your site lives, cloud consumption is metered usage, third-party fees go to vendors, and maintenance keeps everything updated. We itemize each in your proposal.

Do you require long contracts?

No. Development is fixed-scope per proposal. Subscriptions are monthly or annual — you choose. Support is optional; you own the system either way.

How do we start?

Start a project inquiry and pick the service — Starter plans convert straight into intake. For Growth or Premium, choose the consultation path and we'll scope a fixed quote with you.
