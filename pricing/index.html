FAQ

Common questions.

Project development fee vs. everything else?

The development fee pays for design and build work. Hosting is where your site lives, cloud consumption is metered usage, third-party fees go to vendors, and maintenance keeps everything updated. We itemize each in your proposal.

What do the “starting at” prices include?

Each card lists inclusions, optional upgrades, and exclusions. Starting prices assume the smallest useful scope. They exclude hosting, cloud usage, third-party licenses, and ongoing support unless explicitly listed.

How does ongoing support work?

Support plans are quoted per system and shown as Custom Quote. Support is always optional; you own the system either way.

Do you require long contracts?

No. Development is fixed-scope per proposal. Support is optional and explained up front.

How do we start?

Send a project inquiry in plain language → discovery conversation (no commitment) → written proposal with scope, timeline, and cost.
